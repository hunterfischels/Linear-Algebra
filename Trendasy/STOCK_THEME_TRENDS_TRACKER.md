## Executive summary — the meta-narrative

Two forces explain almost every winning subsector below:

1. **The AI infrastructure buildout** — hyperscaler/frontier-lab capex going vertical (OpenAI's
   ~$500B "Stargate," $100B+ custom-silicon commitments, GB200/GB300 → Vera Rubin ramps). This
   pulled gains *up the entire stack*: AI chips → memory (HBM) → optical interconnect (photonics)
   → and, critically, **the power to run it all** (nuclear/SMR/uranium).
2. **Government as a market-mover** — a striking feature of this cycle is how often **policy and
   contracts**, not earnings, set prices: Trump executive orders (drones, nuclear, Golden Dome,
   quantum), the **DoD equity stake in MP Materials**, China's rare-earth export controls,
   Palantir's ~$10B Army deal, CHIPS Act equity stakes in chip & quantum firms, and NATO's 5%
   spending pledge. Several themes (drones, rare earths, quantum, space-defense) are best described
   as **"policy trades."**

**Rough relative-performance pecking order of the themes (2024 → mid-2026), most explosive first:**

| Tier | Themes | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Parabolic / speculative | **Quantum**, **Rare earths**, **small-cap Drones**, **SMR/nuclear developers** | Policy & milestone-driven; multi-hundred-% moves; violent two-way volatility |

## 1) Semiconductors / AI compute chips

### Narrative
AI compute chips are the single most powerful equity theme of this cycle because hyperscaler and
frontier-lab capex on AI data centers has gone vertical, turning chipmakers into direct
beneficiaries of a multi-hundred-billion-dollar infrastructure buildout. **NVIDIA became the
world's first $4 trillion (Jul 2025) and then $5 trillion (Oct 2025) company**, validating that
GPU/accelerator demand is supply-constrained rather than demand-constrained. The theme broadened in
2025–2026 from NVIDIA's near-monopoly toward **custom ASICs** (Broadcom/Marvell winning OpenAI,
Anthropic, hyperscaler designs) and the **"picks-and-shovels" layer** (TSMC foundry, ASML
lithography, ARM IP). Relative outperformance was driven by AI capex/contract announcements,
custom-ASIC design wins, **US-China export-control policy whipsaws** (H20 ban → reversal → H200
approval), and repeated earnings beats with data-center revenue ~doubling YoY. The main risk
overhang — the Jan 2025 DeepSeek "efficient AI" scare — proved transient as capex kept
accelerating.

## 2) Memory chips (DRAM / HBM / NAND)

### Narrative
Memory flipped from the most commoditized, boom-bust corner of semis into its **hottest
AI-leveraged trade**, with **HBM (high-bandwidth memory)** the spearhead — every Nvidia/AMD AI
accelerator consumes large, rising HBM content. A 2024 recovery escalated into a full **"supercycle"**
in 2025–2026: makers diverted DRAM wafer capacity into high-margin HBM (HBM uses ~3× the wafers per
bit), **starving conventional DRAM and NAND supply** just as demand exploded. Pricing tells it:
conventional DRAM contract prices +~18–23% QoQ in Q4 2025 (server DRAM +43–48%), then an
extraordinary **~90–95% QoQ jump in Q1 2026**, NAND +~55–60%; suppliers report being **sold out of
HBM and most DRAM/NAND through 2026 into 2027**. **SK Hynix and Micron both crossed $1 trillion
market cap for the first time.** The risk: it's still a cyclical commodity at heart, and the
violence of the move (SK Hynix ~+1,000% in 12 months) raises late-cycle questions.

## 3) Photonics / optical networking / silicon photonics / co-packaged optics

### Narrative
Optical interconnect became one of the **highest-beta corners of the AI trade** because copper hit
a physical wall: scaling GPU clusters into hundreds of thousands of accelerators requires optics,
and bandwidth-per-port jumped 400G → 800G (2024–25) → **1.6T** (ramping late-2025 into 2026). The
decisive catalyst was Nvidia's **March 2025 GTC** unveiling of **Quantum-X** (InfiniBand) and
**Spectrum-X Photonics** (Ethernet) **co-packaged optics (CPO)** switches — integrating silicon
photonics into the switch ASIC (4× fewer lasers, 3.5× power efficiency) — legitimizing CPO and
pulling forward the whole supply chain. It re-rated again in **March 2026** when **Nvidia invested
$4B ($2B Coherent + $2B Lumentum)**, hard-wiring the two largest optics names into its roadmap.
Result: an extraordinary 2026 melt-up in optics equities, often **outpacing the GPU names**, with
violent two-way single-day swings.

## 4) Drones / UAS / counter-UAS

### Narrative
Drones became one of the hottest **policy-driven** micro-themes, powered by Ukraine battlefield
demand and a US push to onshore production. The Pentagon's **Replicator** initiative ($500M/yr
FY24–25) signaled mass procurement of attritable autonomous systems; **Trump's June 6, 2025 EO
14307 ("Unleashing American Drone Dominance")** made domestic UAS scale-up a presidential priority,
paired with BVLOS rulemaking and counter-UAS airspace orders. The regulatory squeeze on China's DJI
— culminating in the **FCC's Dec 2025 decision (via NDAA FY25 §1709)** to add all foreign-made
drones to its Covered List — created a structural tailwind for "Blue UAS"/NDAA-compliant domestic
suppliers. Reports (mid-2025 and again May 2026) that the US government might **take equity stakes**
in US drone makers repeatedly ignited speculative rallies. Small-cap US-made names massively
outperformed — **on policy/contract catalysts more than earnings.**

## 5) Space / launch / satellites

### Narrative
Space became a hot momentum theme on a rare convergence of commercial and government tailwinds. The
biggest catalyst was **"Golden Dome,"** Trump's **Jan 27, 2025 EO** for space-based missile defense,
creating a multi-year, up-to-**$151B** procurement pipeline (SHIELD IDIQ) plus ~$3.2B in early
space-based-interceptor prototype awards. Simultaneously: **commercial launch cadence / reusable
medium-lift** (Rocket Lab's Neutron), **expanding defense space budgets** (SDA Tranche awards, NSSL
Phase 3), **satellite-to-phone (direct-to-cell)** going commercial (AST SpaceMobile signing AT&T
and Verizon), **lunar/NASA CLPS** missions (Intuitive Machines), and a wave of **2025 IPOs**
(Karman, Voyager) plus anticipation of a SpaceX listing.

## Cross-cutting observations

- **Government action was the dominant alpha source in 2025–26.** The largest single-day and
  multi-week moves clustered around **executive orders** (drones Jun'25, nuclear May'25, Golden Dome
  Jan'25, quantum Jun'26), **direct government capital** (DoD→MP Materials, CHIPS Act equity stakes
  in chip & quantum firms, reported drone-maker stakes), **contracts** (Palantir $10B, Red Cat SRR,
  Golden Dome interceptors), and **trade policy** (China rare-earth controls; US chip export
  controls). Several themes are best understood as **policy trades**.
- **One root demand driver — AI compute — fans out across the stack:** chips → memory (HBM) →
  optics (photonics) → power (nuclear). A position in any one is correlated to AI capex headlines.
- **Speculative tiers detached from fundamentals.** Quantum, micro-cap drones, junior rare-earth and
  pre-revenue SMR names delivered the biggest percentage gains but on milestones/policy, not
  earnings — and exhibit violent two-way volatility (e.g., optics names ±10–24% in single sessions;
  quantum names −25%/+25% swings).
- **Relative rotation within themes.** Watch the laggards-that-aren't-beneficiaries: IPG Photonics
  (industrial lasers, not AI optics), legacy primes (LMT/RTX vs Palantir), and AeroVironment (the
  2026 drone laggard on an EPS miss) all show that the *narrative fit* matters more than the sector
  label.
- **Late-cycle risk.** Memory (~+1,000% SK Hynix) and several speculative names had blow-off moves in
  late 2025; some (PLTR, Rheinmetall, OKLO, rare earths) pulled back 25–46% from peaks by mid-2026.
